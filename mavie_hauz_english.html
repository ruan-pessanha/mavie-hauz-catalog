About Mavie Hauz

Smart doesn't mean complicated.
It means comfortable.

Most people think a smart home requires a massive renovation, messy wires, and thousands of dollars. We believe technology should be invisible — until the moment you need it.

Mavie Hauz was built around one idea: your home should feel warmer, safer, and easier to live in, without a single wall being opened. We curate lighting, security, climate and connectivity devices that actually work together — so a Tuesday evening feels a little more like magic.

We're not a warehouse of random gadgets. Every product on Mavie Hauz is chosen because it solves a real, everyday problem.

Return Policy

What's covered

We offer a full refund or free replacement if your order arrives in any of these conditions:

  • Damaged in transit
  • Defective or not working correctly
  • Wrong item, size or color sent
  • Item missing from your order

To claim, contact us through our page within 30 days of delivery with your order number and clear photos or video of the issue. In most cases you won't need to send the item back.

What's not covered

We're not able to offer refunds for orders that arrive correctly and simply weren't what you expected (change of mind). We know that's not what every store says, but we'd rather be upfront about it than promise something we can't deliver on.

Time limit

Claims must be submitted within 30 days of delivery, and within 90 days of the original order date.
